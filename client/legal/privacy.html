Privacy Policy

Utopian Contributors LLC · Effective 16 November 2025 · Section 9 updated 30 September 2026

At Utopian Contributors, we are profoundly committed to protecting your personal data and ensuring your privacy. This policy outlines our protective stance, how we collect, use, and safeguard your data, and your rights under the European Union’s General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act/California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA).

1. Principles of Data Protection and Transparency

We adhere to the following principles to ensure the highest standard of data protection:

  • Fairness and lawfulness: Processing data only where we have a clear legal basis and with the user’s full knowledge.
  • Purpose limitation: Collecting data only for specific, explicit, and legitimate purposes clearly stated below.
  • Data minimization: Collecting only the data strictly necessary for our stated purposes.
  • Accuracy: Maintaining accurate and up-to-date data.
  • Storage limitation: Retaining data only as long as necessary for the stated purposes.
  • Integrity and confidentiality: Implementing robust security measures to protect against unauthorized or unlawful processing and accidental loss.

2. Data We Collect and Our Lawful Basis for Processing

We collect minimal data necessary to provide our service. Not every category applies to every part of the Service — see section 9 for what UtopianGO itself collects.

  • Identity data — name, email address. Collected for account creation, essential service communication, and identity verification. Lawful basis: contract (necessary for service).
  • Blockchain data — crypto wallet address, whether a wallet you connect or one we hold for your account (see section 9). Collected to facilitate decentralized transactions and service contributions. Lawful basis: contract (necessary for service).
  • Usage data — IP address, device type, browser information, pages visited, time spent on the app/site. Collected for essential operational analytics (security, performance) and to fulfill our legitimate interest in improving the user experience. Lawful basis: legitimate interest (service improvement and security).
  • Transaction data — no direct collection of payment card information; this data is handled by our third-party processor. Processed to process and verify transactions. Lawful basis: contract (necessary for service).

3. How We Use Your Personal Data

We use your data only for the purposes explicitly listed below. We will never process your data for new, incompatible purposes without informing you and, where required, obtaining a new consent.

  • To provide and maintain the Service: to create and manage your account and facilitate contributions.
  • Security and fraud prevention: to monitor for and prevent malicious activity.
  • Service improvement (analytics): to understand how our services are used to make informed improvements. This is only performed using anonymized or aggregated data where possible.
  • Legal compliance: to comply with all applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

4. Strict Data Sharing and Disclosure

Your data is not for sale. We do not sell or share your personal information to third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising. We only disclose your data under the following highly restricted conditions:

  • Payment processors — to securely process financial transactions. Data is tokenized; we only share the minimum required for the transaction, and the processor is contractually obligated to meet GDPR/CCPA standards.
  • Analytics providers — to understand service usage and performance. Data is shared only in anonymized or pseudonymized form to prevent re-identification.
  • Legal obligations — when required by law or to protect our rights (e.g. a court order). We challenge any overbroad request and notify the user unless legally prohibited.
  • Service providers needed to answer your request — the search, price, and routing providers listed in section 9, each of which receives only what it needs to answer the request you made.

5. Your Data Protection Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

You have extensive rights over your personal data. We are committed to honoring these requests promptly and without undue delay.

  • Right to know/access (EU & California) — to know what personal data is being collected and to obtain a copy of the specific pieces of data collected.
  • Right to deletion (EU & California) — to request the deletion of your personal data (the right to be forgotten).
  • Right to correction (EU & California) — to rectify inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to object/opt-out (EU & California) — to object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information (even though we do not sell or share).
  • Right to data portability (EU) — to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.
  • Right to non-discrimination (California) — not to be discriminated against for exercising your privacy rights.

How to exercise your rights: submit a verifiable request to our Data Protection Officer at ludwig@utopiancontrib.com.

6. Data Security and Retention

  • Security measures: We use advanced technical and organizational measures, including encryption (in transit and at rest), pseudonymization, and strict access controls to protect your data from unauthorized access or disclosure.
  • Retention: We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected or as required by law. For account holders, data is generally retained for the life of the account plus a short reconciliation period (e.g. 90 days) after closure, after which it is securely deleted.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on our website and updating the effective date at the top of this document. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

9. UtopianGO

This section is specific to UtopianGO at utopiango.com and describes exactly what the site does with what you type into it. Where it is more specific than the sections above, it governs.

9.1. No trackers, one cookie. UtopianGO loads no analytics or advertising scripts of any kind, and nothing on the site follows you to another one. The only cookie it sets is the session cookie that keeps you signed in to an account. It cannot be read by scripts on the page, lasts 30 days, and is removed when you log out. You can search without an account and without any cookie.

9.2. Your search goes to Brave. To answer a search we forward your query to the Brave Search API from our server, so your query reaches Brave but your IP address and browser do not. We do not write your queries to any database or profile, and there is no search history tied to your account. Queries do appear in the page URL, and so may show up in the ordinary request logs our hosting provider keeps for a short period, alongside your IP address.

9.3. Image and thumbnail results load from their source. Pictures on the Images tab, and thumbnails in video and news results, are fetched by your browser directly from Brave’s image servers or the publisher, which means those hosts see your IP address and user agent. Following any result link takes you to a third party under its own privacy policy, not ours.

9.4. Prices. Token names, prices, and 24-hour charts come from Jupiter and Helius, requested by our server on a schedule rather than per visitor. Looking at a price card sends nothing about you anywhere.

9.5. If you create an account. We ask for no name and no email address. We store:

  • your username, which is permanent;
  • your password, only as a salted scrypt hash from which it cannot be read back;
  • if you add a passkey, its public credential (never its private half, which stays on your device);
  • what you choose to add: a profile photo, bio, and location, your posts and their photos and voice memos, comments, re-posts, the posts you save, and your friends;
  • what the service needs to run: notifications, view counts on posts, when your account was created, and which version of the Terms of Service you accepted and when you accepted it.

9.6. Public content. Your profile (username, photo, bio, and location), posts, post photos, voice memos, re-posts, and comments are public. Anyone can see and play them at your profile page, with or without an account, and anyone can copy them, share them, or have them indexed by search engines. Saved posts and your friends list are visible only to you.

9.7. Messages are stored only encrypted. Direct messages and the photos sent with them are encrypted on your device before they are sent, and we store them only in that encrypted form. Your private messaging key is itself encrypted under your passkey before it reaches us, so we cannot decrypt your messages and cannot hand anyone a readable copy of them. To deliver them we do keep, unencrypted: who sent each message, to whom, when, how many photos it carries, and whether it has been read, as well as each person’s public messaging keys. We keep the most recent 1,000 messages in each conversation and delete older ones automatically.

9.8. Your account’s wallet is custodial. Each account comes with a Solana wallet whose keys are held by Utopian Contributors LLC. Its recovery phrase is stored encrypted twice: once under your password, so that you can view it, and once under a server key kept outside our database, so that our server can sign the transactions you ask for. Our API never returns a private key, and returns the phrase only to you, after you enter your password or use your passkey. The wallet’s address is public. We read its balances from the Solana blockchain via Helius. When you trade or send from it, our server builds the transaction, with Jupiter for a swap, and submits it to Solana. Jupiter and Helius then see your wallet address and our server’s IP address, not yours.

9.9. If you connect an outside wallet. Connecting one is entirely your choice and nothing happens until you do. Its address is then sent to our server so we can read what the address holds, via Helius, and we do not store the result. When you request a quote or a swap with it, your address is sent from your browser directly to Jupiter’s API, which sees your address and your IP. That wallet’s keys never leave it, and it signs and submits the transaction, not us.

9.10. Blockchain data is permanent. Anything that settles on Solana, from either kind of wallet, is public and permanent by the nature of a blockchain. It is outside our control and outside the deletion right in section 5.

9.11. What is stored on your device. Your browser keeps, for this site only:

  • the session cookie in 9.1, while you are signed in;
  • your username, so the header can show it before the page loads;
  • the address and wallet name of an outside wallet you connected;
  • search results you save, and the sites you left search for;
  • your result language, if you picked one;
  • your messaging key, so you are not asked for your passkey every time.

Saved results, the list of sites you visited, and your language choice are never sent to our server. Logging out removes the session cookie and your messaging key. Clearing your browser’s data for this site removes all of it.

9.12. What we keep, and deleting it. We keep your account data for as long as the account exists. To limit abuse, our server counts requests per IP address in memory, and does not write those counts anywhere. There is not yet a button to delete an account. To delete yours, or any post or comment, email the address in section 8. We will ask you to prove you control the account, then delete it and everything listed in 9.5 and 9.7. We cannot recall copies of public content that others have already made, or anything recorded on the blockchain.

9.13. Voice memos and your microphone. A post can carry a voice memo of up to three minutes. Your browser asks for your permission before the site can use your microphone, and only UtopianGO’s social pages can ask; search and the wallet page cannot. The microphone is on only while you are recording, and turns off as soon as you stop, cancel, or close the recorder. The recording is made in your browser and stays there until you post it, so a memo you discard never reaches us. When you post one, we store the audio file, its length, and a small outline of its loudness that the page draws, and it becomes public content under 9.6. We do not transcribe or otherwise analyse recordings.
