Privacy Policy

Utopian Contributors LLC · Effective 16 November 2025

At Utopian Contributors, we are profoundly committed to protecting your personal data and ensuring your privacy. This policy outlines our protective stance, how we collect, use, and safeguard your data, and your rights under the European Union’s General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act/California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA).

1. Principles of Data Protection and Transparency

We adhere to the following principles to ensure the highest standard of data protection:

  • Fairness and lawfulness: Processing data only where we have a clear legal basis and with the user’s full knowledge.
  • Purpose limitation: Collecting data only for specific, explicit, and legitimate purposes clearly stated below.
  • Data minimization: Collecting only the data strictly necessary for our stated purposes.
  • Accuracy: Maintaining accurate and up-to-date data.
  • Storage limitation: Retaining data only as long as necessary for the stated purposes.
  • Integrity and confidentiality: Implementing robust security measures to protect against unauthorized or unlawful processing and accidental loss.

2. Data We Collect and Our Lawful Basis for Processing

We collect minimal data necessary to provide our service. Not every category applies to every part of the Service — see section 9 for what UtopianGO itself collects.

  • Identity data — name, email address. Collected for account creation, essential service communication, and identity verification. Lawful basis: contract (necessary for service).
  • Blockchain data — non-custodial crypto wallet address. Collected to facilitate decentralized transactions and service contributions. Lawful basis: contract (necessary for service).
  • Usage data — IP address, device type, browser information, pages visited, time spent on the app/site. Collected for essential operational analytics (security, performance) and to fulfill our legitimate interest in improving the user experience. Lawful basis: legitimate interest (service improvement and security).
  • Transaction data — no direct collection of payment card information; this data is handled by our third-party processor. Processed to process and verify transactions. Lawful basis: contract (necessary for service).

3. How We Use Your Personal Data

We use your data only for the purposes explicitly listed below. We will never process your data for new, incompatible purposes without informing you and, where required, obtaining a new consent.

  • To provide and maintain the Service: to create and manage your account and facilitate contributions.
  • Security and fraud prevention: to monitor for and prevent malicious activity.
  • Service improvement (analytics): to understand how our services are used to make informed improvements. This is only performed using anonymized or aggregated data where possible.
  • Legal compliance: to comply with all applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

4. Strict Data Sharing and Disclosure

Your data is not for sale. We do not sell or share your personal information to third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising. We only disclose your data under the following highly restricted conditions:

  • Payment processors — to securely process financial transactions. Data is tokenized; we only share the minimum required for the transaction, and the processor is contractually obligated to meet GDPR/CCPA standards.
  • Analytics providers — to understand service usage and performance. Data is shared only in anonymized or pseudonymized form to prevent re-identification.
  • Legal obligations — when required by law or to protect our rights (e.g. a court order). We challenge any overbroad request and notify the user unless legally prohibited.
  • Service providers needed to answer your request — the search, price, and routing providers listed in section 9, each of which receives only what it needs to answer the request you made.

5. Your Data Protection Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

You have extensive rights over your personal data. We are committed to honoring these requests promptly and without undue delay.

  • Right to know/access (EU & California) — to know what personal data is being collected and to obtain a copy of the specific pieces of data collected.
  • Right to deletion (EU & California) — to request the deletion of your personal data (the right to be forgotten).
  • Right to correction (EU & California) — to rectify inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to object/opt-out (EU & California) — to object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information (even though we do not sell or share).
  • Right to data portability (EU) — to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.
  • Right to non-discrimination (California) — not to be discriminated against for exercising your privacy rights.

How to exercise your rights: submit a verifiable request to our Data Protection Officer at ludwig@utopiancontrib.com.

6. Data Security and Retention

  • Security measures: We use advanced technical and organizational measures, including encryption (in transit and at rest), pseudonymization, and strict access controls to protect your data from unauthorized access or disclosure.
  • Retention: We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected or as required by law. For account holders, data is generally retained for the life of the account plus a short reconciliation period (e.g. 90 days) after closure, after which it is securely deleted.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on our website and updating the effective date at the top of this document. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

9. UtopianGO

This section is specific to UtopianGO at utopiango.com and describes exactly what the site does with what you type into it.

9.1. No account, no cookies, no trackers. UtopianGO has no sign-up and no password, so the identity data in section 2 is never collected here. It sets no cookies and loads no analytics or advertising scripts of any kind. There is nothing on the site that follows you to another one. Connecting a wallet is not an account; the one thing the site stores on your device is described in 9.6.

9.2. Your search goes to Brave. To answer a search we forward your query to the Brave Search API from our server, so your query reaches Brave but your IP address and browser do not. We do not write your queries to any database or profile, and there is no search history to log in to. Queries do appear in the page URL, and so may show up in the ordinary request logs our hosting provider keeps for a short period, alongside your IP address.

9.3. Image and thumbnail results load from their source. Pictures on the Images tab, and thumbnails in video and news results, are fetched by your browser directly from Brave’s image servers or the publisher, which means those hosts see your IP address and user agent. Following any result link takes you to a third party under its own privacy policy, not ours.

9.4. Prices. Token names, prices, and 24-hour charts come from Jupiter and Helius, requested by our server on a schedule rather than per visitor. Looking at a price card sends nothing about you anywhere.

9.5. If you connect a wallet. Connecting is entirely your choice and nothing happens until you do. Your wallet address is then sent to our server to read what the address holds — the balances a trade can be funded with, and, on the wallet page, the tokens held and what they are worth. We read those from the Solana blockchain via Helius and do not store them. All of it is public on-chain information that any block explorer will show for any address. Your address is also sent, when you request a quote or a swap, from your browser directly to Jupiter’s API, which sees your address and your IP. Your keys never leave your wallet, we never take custody, and the transaction is signed and submitted by your wallet, not by us. Anything that settles on Solana is public and permanent by the nature of a blockchain, and is outside our control and outside the deletion right in section 5.

9.6. What is stored on your device. If you connect a wallet, your browser remembers its public address, and the name of the wallet extension you picked, in that browser’s local storage — so that you are not asked to connect again on every visit. That is the only thing UtopianGO writes to your device. It is not a cookie, it is never attached automatically to any request, and no copy of it is kept on our server. Pressing “Logout” erases it immediately, as does clearing your browser’s data for this site; either way your wallet may still list this site among those it has approved, which you can revoke in the wallet itself.

9.7. What we keep. Nothing that identifies you. The site holds no user records, so a deletion request about UtopianGO will usually be answered with the fact that there was nothing to delete — you are welcome to ask us to confirm that at the address in section 8.
